Session observations

Enable the camera, select yourself, then start a session to collect basic movement samples.
  • Keep the camera fixed and all players fully visible.
  • For doubles, the model attempts to detect up to four people.
  • If players cross, overlap or leave the frame, temporary IDs can switch. Re-select yourself if needed.

Session data

Setup notes & privacy

  • Use landscape orientation, rear camera and a stable tripod or safe elevated surface.
  • Good lighting helps. At long distances, pose landmarks may be less accurate.
  • Video frames are processed locally in the browser and are not uploaded by this app. The AI runtime and pose model are downloaded from public hosting when needed.
  • This is an experimental prototype. It does not yet detect the shuttlecock, shots, court lines or smash speed.
