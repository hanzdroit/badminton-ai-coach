Setup notes

  • Use landscape orientation, rear camera and a stable tripod or safe elevated surface.
  • Keep the entire player visible. At a long distance, pose landmarks may be less accurate.
  • Good lighting helps. Avoid placing the camera where people frequently walk across the view.
  • Live processing runs on your phone. The pose model is downloaded once from Google's model hosting when first used; video frames are not uploaded to an AI server by this app.
